"If I could give the sky its bluest shade,
I'd drape it 'round your shoulders, then cascade
a thousand roses at your feet each morn —
not one of them as lovely as the form

that blinked the night you were born — near and far —
was jealous of the light you bring to earth.
No calendar can hold the weight of your worth.

Happy Birthday, Chikuuuuu.  With all my heart. 🌹"
